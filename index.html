FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?
We have no fixed minimum order quantity for most products. You can order as little as a single piece. Bulk orders receive preferential pricing — contact us to discuss volume discounts.
Do you supply non-standard or custom sizes?
Yes. For many product categories such as seals, sprockets, and couplings, we can source or arrange fabrication of custom sizes. Please share your drawing or specification and we will advise on lead time and pricing.
Which brands do you stock?
We are authorised distributors for several leading brands in the power transmission and sealing space. We also supply quality-tested generic products conforming to IS/DIN/BS standards. Contact us for brand-specific availability.
How quickly can you deliver?
In-stock items are dispatched the same day or next business day. We deliver to Mumbai and surrounding areas within 24 hours. Pan-India courier shipments typically take 2–5 business days depending on location.
Can I get a product catalog?
Yes, you can download our catalog directly from this website. We also send product-specific brochures and technical data sheets on request. Reach us via the quote form or WhatsApp for detailed technical documentation.
